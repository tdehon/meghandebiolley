FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which treatment should I choose?

The descriptions on the Treatments page will help. If you’re still unsure, book a Q&A call or message me on Instagram and we’ll find the right treatment together.

How do I book?

One-to-one treatments are booked online through my Calendly page. For a workshop or group session, please get in touch by email or phone.

How should I prepare?

Arrive a few minutes early so you have time to settle in (free street parking). Try to avoid a heavy meal just before your treatment. Treatments that include the face and scalp may leave your hair slightly oily, so it’s best not to plan anything important straight afterwards.

Is the facial a beauty treatment?

No extractions are involved: it’s a relaxing, lifting and sculpting facial massage that leaves you with a lovely glow and a lifted look. For longer-lasting results, a course of 5 sessions at a reduced rate is available.

Can I give a treatment as a gift?

Yes! Gift vouchers come in printed or electronic form. See the Gift vouchers page for details.

Which languages do you speak?

French, Dutch and English: whichever you prefer.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, with at least 48 hours’ notice. Sessions cancelled less than 48 hours in advance are charged in full.

How can I pay?

Cash, QR code or invoice.

Are there any contraindications?

If you are pregnant, receiving medical treatment or have a particular health condition, please let me know before your session. If in doubt, ask your doctor. These are wellbeing treatments and do not replace medical care.
