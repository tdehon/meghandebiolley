About

Meghan de Biolley

Massage therapist and wellbeing promoter in Uccle, Brussels. Three years of taking care of you.

Before devoting myself to massage, I spent several years working in a communications agency. One day I chose to follow my intuition and change direction: going independent to do what truly lights me up, taking care of others.

For me, massage is much more than a technique: it’s a moment of attention, presence and trust. I like to combine different approaches — body massage, facial massage, lymphatic drainage and Tibetan singing-bowl sound therapy — to offer treatments that soothe both body and mind.

I welcome you in French, Dutch or English.
