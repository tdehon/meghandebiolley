About

Meghan de Biolley

Massage and wellbeing practitioner in Uccle, Brussels, for three years.

For me, massage is much more than a technique: it’s a moment of attention, presence and trust. I work with people who need to breathe, release tension or simply take some time for themselves.

I like to combine different approaches — body massage, facial work, lymphatic drainage and Tibetan singing-bowl sound therapy — to offer complete treatments that soothe both body and mind.
