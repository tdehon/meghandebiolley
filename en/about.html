Hi, I’m Meghan

Before massage, I spent several years in a communications agency. About four years ago I trained in massage in Koh Samui, Thailand, and it quickly became clear: this was what I wanted to do.

In 2025 I went independent. Today I welcome people at home in Uccle for body and facial massage, run workshops and sound healing sessions, and organise one-day retreats with Éclosion.

What I love most? The little messages I get after a session. They always make my day 🤎

Training and experience

  • Massage training in Koh Samui, Thailand
  • Sound healing certification
  • Workshops and events for Sisley Paris, Avène, Delbôve and Arvesta
  • Founder of Éclosion, one-day wellbeing retreats
  • French, Dutch and English

My story in a podcast

I talked about my career change in episode 38 of the Pop Corn Studio podcast (in French): the doubts, the risk, and why I have no regrets.
